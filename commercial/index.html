Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines pour enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

A kitchen stuck in negative pressure is miserable to work in, and guests notice the drafts and the stuffy dining room fast. Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping your kitchen cooking and guests comfortable in the meantime.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Waterbury HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Fast Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling quickly, throughout Waterbury and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back fast. Once it's running, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so it doesn't happen again, and each response comes with full documentation for your facility files.
