Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in New Haven

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected for a season, can take out the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement.
